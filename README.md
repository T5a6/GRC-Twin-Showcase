# 🛡️ GRC Twin
### AI-Powered Regulatory Compliance Intelligence

**Turning regulatory change into traceable, actionable compliance decisions.**

GRC Twin is an AI-enabled Governance, Risk, and Compliance (GRC) platform designed to help financial institutions manage regulatory changes, map requirements to internal policies, identify potential compliance gaps, and coordinate remediation through human-approved workflows.

**Focus:** RegTech · Cybersecurity · GRC · AI Governance · Financial Services

**Regulatory Context:** Central Bank of Bahrain (CBB)

## 🎯 The Challenge

Financial institutions face complex and continuously evolving regulatory requirements.

Compliance teams must regularly:

- Monitor regulatory changes and updates.
- Interpret requirements and determine their applicability.
- Compare obligations against internal policies and controls.
- Identify potential compliance gaps.
- Coordinate remediation, approvals, and supporting evidence.
- Maintain traceability for compliance reviews and audits.

**The challenge:** Transforming extensive manual regulatory review into an efficient, accountable, and traceable compliance process.

## 💡 Our Solution

GRC Twin brings these activities into a unified AI-assisted compliance workflow.

| Capability | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 🔎 Regulatory Change Analysis | Identify and compare regulatory updates |
| 📑 Requirement Interpretation | Organize and assess regulatory obligations |
| 🔗 Policy & Control Mapping | Connect requirements with internal policies and controls |
| ⚠️ Gap Identification | Highlight potential areas of non-compliance |
| ✅ Human Review & Approval | Validate AI-assisted findings and recommendations |
| 📋 Remediation Tracking | Coordinate actions, responsibilities, and evidence |
| 🕒 Audit Trail | Maintain traceable records of decisions and activities |

**Our principle: AI recommends. Humans review and approve.**

## 🔐 Security, Governance & AI Oversight

GRC Twin was designed with the security and accountability needs of regulated financial institutions in mind.

| Area | Design Focus |
|---|---|
| **Role-Based Access Control** | Separation of administrative, reviewer, and auditor responsibilities |
| **Human-in-the-Loop AI** | Human validation of consequential AI-generated findings |
| **Auditability** | Traceable decisions, approvals, and evidence |
| **AI Governance** | Reviewability, source references, and human accountability |
| **Technology Risk** | Security, access management, and operational considerations |
| **Data Residency** | Assessment of deployment options and data-governance requirements |
| **Deployment Flexibility** | Consideration of managed cloud, bank-owned cloud, and on-premises environments |

These reflect the project's demonstrated workflows and design considerations; they do not represent production certification or regulatory approval.
